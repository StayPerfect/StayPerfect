Why Andalusia?

A region full of contrasts.

Andalusia brings together some of southern Europe's most iconic travel experiences. Historic cities, Moorish palaces, Mediterranean coastlines, mountains, white villages and vibrant local traditions make every part of the region feel different.

From the streets of Sevilla to the Alhambra in Granada and the beaches of the Costa del Sol, Andalusia is ideal for travellers who want to combine culture, food, sunshine, nature and unforgettable experiences.

🏛️ Culture Palaces, cathedrals and historic cities
🏖️ Beaches Costa del Sol, Cádiz and Atlantic coast
🍷 Food Tapas, seafood, olive oil and local wines
💃 Tradition Flamenco, festivals and Andalusian culture

Andalusian Coast

Beaches, sunshine & Mediterranean life

Andalusia has two very different coastlines, from the Mediterranean beaches of the Costa del Sol to the wide Atlantic beaches of Cádiz.

☀️ Costa del Sol

Explore Málaga, Marbella, Nerja and the coastal towns of southern Andalusia. Expect Mediterranean beaches, beach clubs, restaurants, marinas and plenty of sunshine.

🌊 Cádiz & Atlantic Coast

The Atlantic coast offers long sandy beaches, historic coastal towns, fresh seafood, surfing opportunities and spectacular sunsets.

Taste Andalusia

A destination for food lovers.

Andalusian cuisine is deeply connected to the Mediterranean landscape. Olive oil, fresh seafood, vegetables, cured meats and local wines are part of everyday life.

Food is also a way to explore the region. Every city and province has its own specialties and traditions.

🍅 Gazpacho & Salmorejo
🥘 Traditional Andalusian dishes
🐟 Fresh seafood & Atlantic cuisine
🫒 Andalusian olive oil
🍷 Sherry & regional wines
Nature & Adventure

Andalusia beyond the cities.

The region is much more than historic cities. Mountains, forests, white villages, protected landscapes and dramatic coastlines offer plenty of opportunities for outdoor adventures.

⛰️ Sierra Nevada

Mountain landscapes, hiking routes and spectacular views near Granada.

🌿 Andalusian Countryside

Olive groves, rolling hills, forests and peaceful rural landscapes.

🌊 Coastal Adventures

Surfing, sailing, hiking and exploring the Mediterranean and Atlantic coastlines.

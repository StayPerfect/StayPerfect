Why Menorca?

A slower side of the Balearics.

Menorca offers a different rhythm from the better-known party islands. Its turquoise coves, rural landscapes, traditional villages and coastal paths make it especially attractive to travellers looking for nature, relaxation and authentic Mediterranean experiences.

The island is also a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and is known for its rich archaeological heritage, making it possible to combine beaches and outdoor adventures with history and culture.

🏖️ Beaches Turquoise coves and Mediterranean swimming
🌿 Nature Coastal landscapes, countryside and protected areas
🏛️ Heritage Talayotic monuments and historic towns
🌅 Slow Travel Peaceful days, local food and island traditions

City & Culture

Ciutadella & Menorca's historic side

Ciutadella is one of the island's most atmospheric towns, with historic streets, traditional architecture, plazas, restaurants and a beautiful harbour setting.

On the eastern side of the island, Maó is another important centre, known for its large natural harbour and connection with Menorca's maritime history.

Beyond the towns, Menorca's prehistoric Talayotic heritage adds another dimension to the island, with archaeological sites spread across the landscape.

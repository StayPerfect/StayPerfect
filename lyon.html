Why Lyon?

A city where history and gastronomy meet.

Lyon brings together Roman ruins, Renaissance architecture, historic neighbourhoods, two rivers, creative districts and one of France's most celebrated food cultures.

Whether you are visiting for gastronomy, history, architecture, culture, a romantic city break or an authentic French experience, Lyon offers a different side of France from the country's better known destinations.

🏛️ UNESCO Heritage Historic Lyon recognised as World Heritage
🍷 Gastronomy Bouchons, markets and Lyonnaise specialities
🧵 Silk Heritage Discover the history of Lyon's silk workers
🌊 Two Rivers Saône and Rhône shape the city

Explore Lyon

Which side of Lyon is for you?

Lyon's districts each reveal a different side of the city, from Renaissance streets and Roman heritage to silk history, shopping and contemporary architecture.

🏘️ Vieux Lyon

Explore one of Europe's largest Renaissance districts through narrow streets, historic courtyards, Saint-Jean Cathedral, traditional bouchons and famous hidden traboules.

Explore Vieux Lyon →

⛪ Fourvière

Climb Lyon's historic hill for the Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourvière, Roman theatres, ancient heritage and spectacular panoramic views over the city.

Explore Fourvière →

🛍️ Presqu'île

The central peninsula between the Rhône and Saône is perfect for shopping, elegant streets, historic squares, cafés, restaurants and city life.

Explore Presqu'île →

🧵 Croix-Rousse

Discover the former silk workers' hill, colourful streets, creative boutiques, cafés, street art, traboules and the distinctive atmosphere of the canuts.

Explore Croix-Rousse →

🏙️ Confluence

Experience contemporary Lyon at the meeting point of the Rhône and Saône, with modern architecture, cultural spaces, waterfront walks and a very different urban atmosphere.

Explore Confluence →

🎨 Terreaux

Explore Place des Terreaux, museums, architecture, cafés and the lively northern gateway to the Presqu'île and Croix-Rousse.

Explore Terreaux →

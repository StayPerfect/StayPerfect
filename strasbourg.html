Why Strasbourg?

A beautiful meeting point between France and Alsace.

Strasbourg is one of France's most distinctive city destinations, combining medieval streets, canals, timber-framed houses, French gastronomy and strong Alsatian traditions.

Whether you are visiting for Christmas markets, a romantic weekend, architecture, food, culture, family travel or a relaxed European city break, Strasbourg offers a memorable experience.

⛪ Strasbourg Cathedral A spectacular Gothic landmark
🏘️ Petite France Canals and historic Alsatian houses
🍷 Alsatian Food Regional dishes, wine and local traditions
🎄 Christmas Markets One of Europe's famous festive destinations

Why Gran Canaria?

An island of surprising variety.

Gran Canaria combines different landscapes in a relatively compact destination. You can spend the morning on the coast, drive into the mountains for dramatic views and finish the day beside the Atlantic.

Its combination of beaches, outdoor activities, resorts, villages and restaurants makes it one of the most flexible Canary Islands for different types of travellers.

✓ Excellent choice for beach holidays
✓ Great variety of outdoor activities
✓ Suitable for couples, families, friends and solo travellers
✓ Easy to combine coast, mountains and culture

Iconic Gran Canaria

Maspalomas Dunes 🏜️

The Maspalomas dunes are one of Gran Canaria's most recognisable natural landscapes, where golden sand meets the Atlantic Ocean.

The surrounding area also offers beaches, restaurants, accommodation and easy access to some of the island's most popular experiences.

Where to stay

Different areas, different Gran Canaria

Choosing the right base can completely change your experience of the island.

🏖️

Maspalomas

A strong choice for beaches, dunes, resorts, restaurants and a classic Gran Canaria holiday atmosphere.

🌊

Puerto Rico

A sunny southern base with beaches, water activities, restaurants and a lively holiday atmosphere.

🎉

Playa del Inglés

A lively destination with nightlife, entertainment, restaurants, shopping and easy access to the coast.

🏙️

Las Palmas

The island's main city, ideal for travellers who want urban life, culture, restaurants and city beaches.

🌿

Puerto de Mogán

A picturesque coastal area with canals, restaurants, harbour views and a more relaxed atmosphere.

🏔️

Tejeda

An inland mountain destination for travellers looking for landscapes, hiking and a quieter side of Gran Canaria.

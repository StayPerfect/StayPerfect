Why Cabrera?

A wild Mediterranean island protected by nature.

Cabrera is a small archipelago located south of Mallorca and one of the most remarkable natural destinations in the Balearics. The islands are protected as the Cabrera Archipelago Maritime-Terrestrial National Park.

The destination combines beautiful beaches, clear Mediterranean waters, rugged coastlines, hiking routes, wildlife and a fascinating history. It is ideal for travellers looking for a quieter and more natural side of Mallorca.

🌿 National Park Protected land and marine ecosystems
🌊 Clear Waters Swimming, snorkelling and marine life
🥾 Hiking Trails, viewpoints and historic sites
💙 Cova Blava The famous Blue Cave of Cabrera

Cabrera Highlights

A protected Mediterranean world.

The Cabrera Archipelago Maritime-Terrestrial National Park protects a spectacular combination of islands, coastline and surrounding sea. It became a national park in 1991 and is one of Spain's most important protected Mediterranean areas.

The island's natural harbour, historic castle, beaches, hiking routes and marine environment make Cabrera a destination where nature and history come together.

🏰 Cabrera Castle Historic fortress above the harbour
💙 Cova Blava Famous Blue Cave
🏖️ Sa Platgeta Beautiful protected beach area
🐟 Marine Park Exceptional underwater environment
History & Heritage

More than just a beautiful island.

Cabrera also has a fascinating human history. Its strategic position in the Mediterranean has influenced the island for centuries, while the castle above the harbour remains one of its most recognisable landmarks.

The island was also used as a prison camp for French soldiers during the Napoleonic period. Today, its historic sites form an important part of the experience alongside the protected natural environment.

🏰 Castle Historic defensive structure
⚓ Harbour Natural sheltered port
📜 History Centuries of Mediterranean heritage
🌿 Conservation Nature remains the priority

Why Lanzarote

An island unlike anywhere else.

Lanzarote combines volcanic landscapes, Atlantic beaches, dramatic coastlines and a relaxed island atmosphere. Its scenery makes it one of the most distinctive destinations in the Canary Islands.

From Timanfaya's volcanic terrain to the beaches of Papagayo and the vineyards of La Geria, Lanzarote offers an impressive variety of experiences in a relatively compact island.

  • Volcanic landscapes and national parks
  • Beautiful beaches and Atlantic coastline
  • Surfing and outdoor adventures
  • Unique architecture and island culture
  • Excellent food and local wine

Places to explore

Discover Lanzarote

Different parts of the island offer different experiences, from beaches and resorts to volcanic landscapes and peaceful villages.

Timanfaya

A spectacular volcanic landscape and one of Lanzarote's defining natural attractions.

Playa Papagayo

Clear water, golden sand and dramatic coastal scenery in the south of the island.

Jameos del Agua

A remarkable volcanic cave system transformed into one of the island's iconic cultural spaces.

Costa Teguise

A popular coastal area with beaches, restaurants, water activities and easy access to the island.

Puerto del Carmen

A lively resort area with beaches, restaurants, nightlife and plenty of accommodation.

La Geria

A unique wine region where vineyards grow in Lanzarote's volcanic soil.

When should you visit Lanzarote?

Lanzarote has a mild climate and can work well throughout the year, with different seasons offering slightly different experiences.

🌸 Spring Comfortable temperatures and excellent conditions for exploring outdoors.
☀️ Summer Warm, bright and ideal for beach days and water activities.

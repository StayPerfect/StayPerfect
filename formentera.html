Why Formentera?

Small island, spectacular Mediterranean scenery.

Formentera is one of the most distinctive destinations in the Balearic Islands. Its appeal comes from the combination of clear turquoise water, sandy beaches, rocky coastlines, Mediterranean vegetation and a relaxed island atmosphere.

The island is compact enough to explore at a slower pace, making it particularly attractive for travellers who want to combine beach time with cycling, walking, photography, nature and quiet coastal discoveries.

🏖️ White Beaches Long sandy beaches and beautiful coves
🌊 Turquoise Water Clear Mediterranean sea and swimming
🚲 Cycling Explore the island at your own pace
🌅 Sunsets Exceptional evening views over the sea

Formentera Highlights

Places worth discovering

From famous beaches to dramatic viewpoints, Formentera offers a compact collection of memorable Mediterranean landscapes.

Beach

Ses Illetes

One of Formentera's most recognisable coastal landscapes, known for pale sand and exceptionally clear turquoise water.

South Coast

Playa de Migjorn

A long stretch of southern coastline with sandy beaches, rocky sections, coves and beautiful sea views.

Cove

Caló des Mort

A small and scenic cove surrounded by rugged coastal scenery, particularly attractive for travellers seeking a quieter setting.

Southwest Formentera

Cap de Barbaria

A dramatic southern landscape known for its lighthouse, rocky coastline and open views across the Mediterranean.

Seaside

Es Pujols

One of the island's best-known seaside areas, with restaurants, shops, accommodation and easy access to the coast.

Eastern Formentera

La Mola

The elevated eastern part of the island offers panoramic landscapes, rural scenery and spectacular views towards the sea.

Mediterranean Nature

Nature & landscapes

Formentera is more than a beach destination. Its inland landscape, coastal ecosystems and protected areas add another dimension to the island experience.

🌿

Mediterranean Vegetation

Explore pine, juniper, shrubs and dry Mediterranean landscapes across the island.

🌊

Clear Waters

The sea surrounding Formentera is one of its defining natural features, with exceptional visibility in many areas.

🐦

Birdlife

Coastal and wetland environments provide important habitats for Mediterranean bird species.

🌾

Rural Landscapes

Discover traditional countryside, dry-stone walls, fields and quiet roads away from the busiest beaches.

🏝️

Coastal Ecosystems

Dunes, beaches, rocky shores and shallow waters create a varied Mediterranean coastal environment.

🌅

Open Horizons

The island's low-rise landscape creates wide views across the Mediterranean and spectacular evening light.

Island Lifestyle

Slow down and enjoy the Mediterranean.

Formentera is particularly appealing when explored without rushing. Its compact size makes it easy to combine beach time with villages, cycling routes, scenic viewpoints and long lunches.

Rather than trying to see everything in one day, allow yourself time to stop at a beach, explore a quiet road, swim in the sea, watch the sunset and enjoy the island atmosphere.

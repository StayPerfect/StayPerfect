Why Algarve?

One coast, many ways to travel.

The Algarve is one of Portugal's best-known coastal regions, famous for its dramatic cliffs, sandy beaches, Atlantic scenery and relaxed southern atmosphere.

Beyond the beaches, the region offers historic towns, fishing villages, nature reserves, golf courses, surfing areas, local food and scenic roads that make it ideal for different types of trips.

🏖️ Beaches Golden cliffs, coves and Atlantic beaches
🌊 Coast Boat trips, viewpoints and coastal walks
⛳ Golf Major golf destinations across the region
🍽️ Food Seafood, regional dishes and local traditions
Explore the Region

Where should you go in the Algarve?

From the dramatic western coast to the quieter eastern Algarve, different towns offer very different holiday experiences.

🇵🇹 Lagos

A popular base for beaches, dramatic cliffs, historic streets, boat trips and access to some of the Algarve's most famous coastline.

🌊 Lagoa & Carvoeiro

A beautiful stretch of coastline known for cliffs, coves, scenic viewpoints and some of the region's most photographed beaches.

☀️ Albufeira

A lively coastal destination with beaches, restaurants, nightlife, family-friendly options and a wide choice of accommodation.

🏛️ Faro

The Algarve's regional capital, offering historic streets, culture, restaurants and access to the Ria Formosa.

🌿 Tavira

A quieter eastern Algarve destination with traditional architecture, riverfront streets, nearby islands and a more relaxed atmosphere.

🌊 Sagres

A wild Atlantic setting popular for surfing, dramatic cliffs, coastal landscapes and outdoor adventures.

Atlantic Coast

Algarve beaches & coastline

The Algarve coastline combines long sandy beaches, hidden coves, dramatic cliffs and spectacular Atlantic viewpoints.

🏖️

Golden Cliffs

The central and western Algarve are famous for orange and golden rock formations rising above the Atlantic.

🌊

Hidden Coves

Small beaches surrounded by cliffs create some of the Algarve's most memorable coastal scenery.

🏄

Surf Beaches

The western Algarve and Atlantic coast around Sagres offer strong conditions and a vibrant surf culture.

⛵

Boat Beaches

Boat excursions provide another way to discover caves, cliffs, sea arches and beaches that are difficult to reach by land.

Outdoor Algarve

Nature & landscapes

The Algarve is much more than beaches, with protected wetlands, cliffs, countryside, coastal paths and diverse natural landscapes.

🥾

Coastal Walks

Follow cliff-top paths and coastal trails for spectacular Atlantic views and access to quieter parts of the coastline.

🌿

Ria Formosa

Explore wetlands, lagoons, islands and wildlife around one of the Algarve's most distinctive natural environments.

🦩

Wildlife

The region's wetlands and coastal environments provide opportunities to discover birds and other local wildlife.

🌊

Atlantic Cliffs

Dramatic rock formations and cliff landscapes define much of the Algarve's western and central coast.

🚶

Outdoor Adventures

Combine walking, cycling, kayaking and other outdoor activities with a coastal holiday.

🌅

Sunset Landscapes

Western-facing beaches and cliffs create memorable Atlantic sunsets, especially around the western Algarve.

Taste the Algarve

Food & local flavours

Algarve cuisine reflects its Atlantic location, fishing traditions, Mediterranean ingredients and Portuguese culinary culture.

🐟

Fresh Fish

Fresh Atlantic fish is central to the region's coastal food culture and can be found in restaurants throughout the Algarve.

🦐

Seafood

Shellfish and seafood dishes are an essential part of the Algarve's traditional coastal cuisine.

🍊

Local Produce

Citrus, almonds, figs, olives and other local ingredients contribute to the region's distinctive flavours.

🍷

Regional Wine

Discover Algarve wine producers and combine tastings with countryside and food experiences.

☕

Cafés

Take a break in local cafés and enjoy a slower side of Algarve life.

🍽️

Local Restaurants

From simple seafood restaurants to contemporary dining, the Algarve offers plenty of ways to explore Portuguese cuisine.

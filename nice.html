Why Nice?

The Mediterranean side of France.

Nice is one of the French Riviera's most attractive destinations, offering a rare combination of city life and Mediterranean relaxation. Its famous seafront, historic centre, markets, restaurants and museums make it easy to enjoy a varied trip without travelling far.

The city is also an excellent base for exploring the Côte d'Azur, with beautiful coastal towns, viewpoints and Riviera destinations within easy reach.

🌊 Mediterranean Beautiful coastline and sea views
🏛️ Old Town Colourful streets and historic architecture
🍷 French Cuisine Markets, restaurants and Riviera flavours
☀️ Sunshine A relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere

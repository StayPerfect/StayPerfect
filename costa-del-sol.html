Why Costa del Sol?

Sun, sea, cities and Andalusian character.

The Costa del Sol stretches along the Mediterranean coast of Málaga province and combines beaches with mountains, historic towns, modern resorts, marinas and traditional Andalusian villages.

It can be a relaxed beach escape, a cultural city break, a romantic holiday, a family trip, a nightlife adventure or a base for exploring the mountains and white villages of inland Andalusia.

☀️ Sunshine A Mediterranean climate and long sunny days
🏖️ Beaches Golden beaches, coves and Mediterranean waters
🏙️ Cities Málaga, Marbella and lively coastal towns
🍷 Food Espetos, pescaíto frito, tapas and Andalusian cuisine

Explore Costa del Sol

Where will the coast take you?

From the cultural energy of Málaga to the glamour of Marbella and the natural beauty of Nerja, each destination has its own personality.

🏙️ Málaga

The cultural heart of the Costa del Sol. Discover the Alcazaba, Gibralfaro Castle, museums, beaches, tapas, shopping and lively neighbourhoods such as Soho.

Explore Málaga →

✨ Marbella

Elegant streets, beaches, luxury hotels, restaurants, golf, Puerto Banús and the charming Old Town make Marbella ideal for a stylish Mediterranean escape.

Explore Marbella →

🌊 Nerja

Known for its dramatic coastline, crystal-clear beaches, Maro coves, the Balcón de Europa and spectacular caves.

Explore Nerja →

🏖️ Fuengirola

A lively seaside destination with a long promenade, beaches, restaurants, family attractions and easy access to other parts of the Costa del Sol.

Explore Fuengirola →

🎉 Torremolinos

A classic Costa del Sol resort known for beaches, chiringuitos, nightlife, restaurants and a lively atmosphere close to Málaga.

Explore Torremolinos →

🏘️ Mijas

A beautiful whitewashed mountain village where traditional Andalusian architecture meets panoramic views towards the coast.

Explore Mijas →

🌸 Estepona

A relaxed coastal town with flower-filled streets, beaches, a charming old centre, restaurants and a slower Mediterranean rhythm.

Explore Estepona →

⛰️ Ronda

One of Andalusia's most spectacular inland destinations, famous for its dramatic gorge, historic centre and iconic bridge.

Explore Ronda →

⛵ Benalmádena

A versatile destination combining beaches, a marina, family attractions, coastal restaurants and a picturesque mountain village.

Explore Benalmádena →

Costa del Sol Experiences

What can you experience?

Build your trip around the experiences you enjoy most.

🏖️

Mediterranean Beaches

Relax on long sandy beaches, discover hidden coves and enjoy the warm Mediterranean atmosphere.

🐟

Espetos & Seafood

Try Málaga's famous espetos and fresh seafood in traditional beachside chiringuitos.

🏛️

Málaga Culture

Discover Picasso, museums, the Alcazaba, Gibralfaro Castle, the historic centre and modern neighbourhoods.

🏘️

White Villages

Explore Mijas, Frigiliana, Casares and other whitewashed Andalusian villages in the mountains.

🥾

Nature & Adventure

Hike, cycle, explore caves and discover the mountains and natural landscapes surrounding the coast.

⛵

Marinas & Sea

Explore Puerto Banús, Benalmádena and other marinas, or experience the coast from the water.

⛳

Golf

The Costa del Sol is famous for its concentration of golf courses and is often associated with the Costa del Golf.

🌅

Sunsets

End the day with Mediterranean sunsets from beaches, rooftops, viewpoints and coastal promenades.

🛍️

Shopping

Combine local markets, charming old-town boutiques, designer shopping and modern retail areas.

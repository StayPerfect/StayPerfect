Why Ibiza?

One island. Many ways to travel.

Ibiza is famous around the world for its nightlife, but the island offers far more than clubs and parties. Turquoise coves, pine-covered hills, whitewashed villages, historic streets, Mediterranean cuisine and spectacular sunsets create a destination with many different personalities.

Whether you want a relaxing escape, a romantic break, a family holiday, an active adventure or unforgettable nights with friends, choosing the right part of Ibiza can completely change your experience.

🏖️ Beaches Turquoise water, sandy beaches and secluded coves.
🌙 Nightlife World-famous clubs, beach venues and evening experiences.
🏛️ Culture Dalt Vila, historic streets and traditional villages.
🌅 Sunsets Memorable Mediterranean sunsets along the west coast.

Mediterranean Coast

Ibiza beaches & coves

From famous beaches to smaller calas, Ibiza offers a wide range of coastal experiences for different types of travellers.

🌊

Cala Comte

A spectacular coastal area known for clear Mediterranean water, small beaches and memorable sunset views.

🏝️

Cala d'Hort

A scenic southwest-coast setting with dramatic sea views and the iconic island landscape of Es Vedrà nearby.

☀️

Talamanca

A convenient beach area close to Ibiza Town, combining the sea with restaurants, walks and easy access to the city.

💙

Hidden Calas

Smaller coves around the island can provide a quieter atmosphere for travellers looking to escape the busiest areas.

City & Culture

Ibiza Town & Dalt Vila

Ibiza Town combines Mediterranean atmosphere, restaurants, shopping, harbour views, nightlife and historic streets.

Above the city, Dalt Vila offers a completely different side of Ibiza, with historic walls, narrow streets, viewpoints and a rich cultural atmosphere.

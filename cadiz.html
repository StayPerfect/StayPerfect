Why Cádiz?

Where Atlantic history meets Andalusian life.

Cádiz is one of Spain's most atmospheric coastal cities. Its historic centre sits on a peninsula surrounded by the Atlantic, creating a destination where the sea is never far away.

The city combines ancient history, elegant squares, colourful neighbourhoods, beaches, seafood restaurants and spectacular Atlantic sunsets.

🌊 Atlantic Coast Ocean views and spectacular seafront walks
🏛️ Ancient History Centuries of Phoenician, Roman and Spanish heritage
🏖️ City Beaches La Caleta, Victoria and Santa María del Mar
🍤 Local Food Fresh seafood, fried fish and traditional tapas
Cádiz Highlights

Places you shouldn't miss

Cádiz is compact enough to explore on foot, with many of its most memorable sights concentrated around the historic centre.

⛪ Cádiz Cathedral

One of the city's defining landmarks, rising above the historic centre and the Campo del Sur waterfront.

🔭 Torre Tavira

Climb one of Cádiz's historic watchtowers for panoramic views over the old town and the Atlantic.

🏘️ Old Town

Wander through narrow streets, historic plazas, colourful facades and atmospheric neighbourhoods such as El Pópulo and La Viña.

Atlantic Cádiz

Walk along Campo del Sur

One of the most memorable ways to experience Cádiz is simply to walk beside the Atlantic, with the historic city rising beside you.

Beaches of Cádiz

Choose your Atlantic beach

From the historic La Caleta to the wide sands of Victoria and the more natural surroundings of Cortadura, Cádiz offers several different beach experiences.

🏖️ La Caleta

The iconic beach of the old town, framed by San Sebastián and Santa Catalina castles and known for its calm atmosphere.

🌊 Playa Victoria

A wide urban Atlantic beach with a long promenade and easy access to restaurants and the modern side of Cádiz.

🌾 Playa de Cortadura

A more natural stretch of coastline with dunes and a different, more open Atlantic atmosphere.

Taste Cádiz

A city made for seafood lovers

Cádiz's Atlantic location gives its cuisine a strong connection with seafood, local markets, traditional tapas and Andalusian flavours.

🍤 Pescaíto Frito Try Cádiz-style fried fish and seafood in traditional neighbourhoods such as La Viña.
🦐 Tortillitas de Camarones A classic local speciality made with small shrimp and a light, crispy batter.
🐟 Seafood & Fish Fresh fish and seafood are central to the culinary identity of the Cádiz coast.
🍷 Sherry & Andalusian Flavours Pair local dishes with wines from the wider Cádiz province and discover the region's distinctive food culture.

Why the Canary Islands?

One archipelago. Completely different worlds.

The Canary Islands combine volcanic landscapes, beaches, mountains, forests, coastal villages and Atlantic culture. Each island has its own personality, making the archipelago particularly interesting for travellers who want choice.

You can spend one trip relaxing on the beach, exploring volcanoes, hiking through forests, cycling mountain roads, surfing Atlantic waves or simply discovering small island villages.

🌋 Volcanoes Dramatic volcanic landscapes
🏖️ Beaches Hundreds of beaches and coves
🌿 Nature Forests, mountains and protected landscapes
☀️ Year-round Escape Mild Atlantic climate

Must See

Canary Islands attractions

From volcanoes and national parks to beaches, historic towns and extraordinary viewpoints, there is something to discover on every island.

🌋 Teide National Park

Tenerife's spectacular volcanic landscape dominated by Mount Teide, Spain's highest peak.

🌋 Timanfaya

Lanzarote's extraordinary volcanic landscape of lava fields, craters and dramatic colours.

🌿 Garajonay

La Gomera's atmospheric laurel forest offers one of the archipelago's most distinctive natural environments.

⛰️ Roque Nublo

One of Gran Canaria's iconic mountain landscapes and a favourite destination for hikers and photographers.

🏜️ Maspalomas

A spectacular combination of dunes, coastline and Atlantic scenery in southern Gran Canaria.

🌲 Caldera de Taburiente

La Palma's dramatic national park combines forests, ravines, mountains and volcanic landscapes.

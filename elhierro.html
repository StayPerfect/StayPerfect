Why El Hierro

A different side of the Canary Islands

El Hierro is ideal for travellers who want nature, tranquillity, adventure and authentic island landscapes rather than large resorts and busy tourist areas.

🌿

Wild & peaceful

Discover a quieter Canary Island where dramatic coastlines, volcanic terrain and green landscapes create a naturally relaxed atmosphere.

🤿

Made for exploration

From hiking and diving to natural swimming pools and scenic drives, El Hierro rewards travellers who enjoy discovering places at their own pace.

🌋

Volcanic character

Volcanic formations, lava landscapes and rugged coastlines give the island a distinctive identity that feels very different from mainland Spain.

🧘

Slow travel

El Hierro works particularly well for travellers looking to disconnect from crowds and enjoy a slower, more peaceful island experience.

Welcome to Madrid

The energetic heart of Spain.

Madrid is a city where historic Spain and modern European life meet. From elegant royal palaces and famous museums to lively plazas, local markets and late-night streets, the capital offers something for every kind of traveler.

Spend your morning discovering masterpieces at the Prado, enjoy lunch with traditional Spanish flavors, walk through Retiro Park in the afternoon and finish the day with tapas and drinks in one of the city's lively neighborhoods.

Whether you're visiting for a weekend or staying longer, Madrid rewards travelers who take their time and explore beyond the main sights.

Must See

Madrid highlights

Start your journey with some of the city's most iconic landmarks and experiences.

Royal Palace

Explore one of Europe's most impressive royal residences and discover magnificent rooms, historic collections and grand architecture.

Prado Museum

Discover one of Europe's great art collections and explore masterpieces from Spain and beyond.

Retiro Park

Escape the city streets in Madrid's famous green oasis, perfect for walking, relaxing and discovering the Crystal Palace and its lake.

Madrid's neighborhoods

Each area of Madrid has its own personality, atmosphere and rhythm.

Centro

The historic heart of Madrid, ideal for first-time visitors and major landmarks.

Malasaña

Creative, youthful and full of independent shops, cafés, bars and nightlife.

Chueca

One of Madrid's most vibrant neighborhoods, known for restaurants, design and nightlife.

La Latina

A historic neighborhood famous for traditional tapas, plazas and Sunday street life.

Salamanca

Elegant streets, luxury shopping, fine dining and beautiful architecture.

Lavapiés

Multicultural, artistic and full of alternative restaurants, galleries and local life.

Travel Tips

Before you go

A few simple tips will help you get the most out of your Madrid trip.

Best time to visit

Spring and autumn are especially pleasant for exploring the city on foot.

Getting around

Madrid has an extensive metro and public transport network, while many central sights are walkable.

How long to stay

Three to four days is a great starting point for discovering Madrid's main attractions.

Take your time

Madrid comes alive later in the day, so leave room for long lunches and evening walks.

Why the Corsican Coast?

A Mediterranean escape unlike any other.

Corsica combines spectacular Mediterranean scenery with a rugged, natural character. Along its coastline you can discover sandy beaches, crystal-clear water, granite cliffs, quiet coves and historic coastal towns.

From the south around Bonifacio and Porto-Vecchio to the west coast and the Gulf of Porto, every section of the island offers a different landscape and atmosphere.

🏖️ Beaches White sand, turquoise water and secluded coves
🌊 Sea Swimming, sailing, diving and boat adventures
⛰️ Cliffs Dramatic coastal landscapes and granite formations
🌅 Sunsets Beautiful Mediterranean evenings by the sea

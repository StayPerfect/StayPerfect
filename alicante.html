Why Alicante?

A city made for Mediterranean living.

Alicante combines the atmosphere of a historic Spanish city with the ease of a beach destination. Its coastline, old quarter, harbour and Santa Bárbara Castle are all closely connected, making it easy to experience several sides of the city in one trip.

The famous Explanada de España, the urban Playa del Postiguet, the old town and the castle are among the city's defining experiences. Alicante is also known for its rice-based cuisine and Mediterranean traditions.

🏖️ Beach Postiguet and Costa Blanca coastline
🏰 History Santa Bárbara Castle and old town
🌴 Lifestyle Promenades, marina and Mediterranean atmosphere
🍚 Food Rice dishes, seafood and local cuisine
Alicante Highlights

Three places to start

Alicante's biggest attractions bring together history, architecture, the sea and everyday Mediterranean life.

🏰 Santa Bárbara Castle

Dominating Mount Benacantil, Santa Bárbara Castle is one of Alicante's defining landmarks and offers spectacular views across the city and bay.

🌴 Explanada de España

Walk beneath the palms along Alicante's famous waterfront promenade, with its distinctive wave-like marble mosaic and views towards the harbour.

🏘️ Old Town & Santa Cruz

Explore colourful streets, historic buildings, churches, small squares and the traditional atmosphere of Alicante's old quarter.

Sea & Sun

Alicante by the sea

One of Alicante's biggest advantages is how easily the city connects with the Mediterranean coast.

🏖️ Playa del Postiguet

The emblematic urban beach of Alicante sits directly below Santa Bárbara Castle and close to the Explanada. Its fine golden sand and calm Mediterranean waters make it easy to combine a beach day with sightseeing.

🌊 Costa Blanca

Alicante is also a natural base for exploring the wider Costa Blanca, with coastal towns, coves, beaches and Mediterranean landscapes beyond the city itself.

Balearic Islands FAQ

Planning your trip

A few useful things to consider before choosing your island.

Which Balearic Island is best for beaches?

Formentera is particularly famous for its turquoise water and beaches. Menorca is another excellent choice for travellers looking for beautiful coves and a quieter atmosphere. Mallorca offers the greatest variety.

Which island is best for nightlife?

Ibiza is the most famous choice for nightlife, but Mallorca also offers a wide selection of bars, restaurants and evening entertainment.

Which Balearic Island is best for families?

Mallorca and Menorca are strong choices because they combine beaches, activities, accommodation options and a broad range of travel styles.

Which island is best for nature?

Menorca, Cabrera and Dragonera are excellent choices for travellers looking for protected landscapes, hiking and quieter surroundings.

Can I visit more than one Balearic Island?

Yes. Travellers can combine islands depending on their itinerary and available transport connections. A multi-island trip can be a great way to experience different sides of the Balearics.

Is Mallorca good for a first trip?

Mallorca is one of the easiest choices for a first Balearic holiday because of its variety. You can combine beaches, mountains, villages, restaurants, culture and activities in one trip.

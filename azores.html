Why the Azores?

An Atlantic world of volcanoes, ocean and nature.

The Azores are a Portuguese archipelago in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean. The islands combine dramatic volcanic scenery, green landscapes, crater lakes, thermal waters and a strong connection with the sea.

Each island has its own personality. São Miguel is the largest and offers an especially broad mix of landscapes and experiences, while islands such as Pico, Faial, Terceira and Flores provide very different reasons to visit.

🌋 Volcanoes Crater lakes, volcanic formations and geothermal landscapes
🌿 Nature Lush scenery, forests, mountains and coastal trails
🐋 Ocean Whale watching, dolphins and Atlantic experiences
♨️ Thermal Natural hot springs and geothermal bathing experiences
Explore the Islands

Which Azores island should you visit?

The archipelago offers different landscapes and travel experiences across its islands.

🌿 São Miguel

The largest island, known for crater lakes, volcanic landscapes, thermal waters, hiking, viewpoints and a wide range of experiences.

🏛️ Terceira

Historic Angra do Heroísmo, volcanic scenery, traditional culture, coastline and a lively island atmosphere.

🌋 Pico

Home to Portugal's highest mountain, dramatic volcanic scenery, vineyards and a strong connection with the Atlantic.

⛵ Faial

A favourite for sailing, volcanic landscapes, ocean views and the iconic marina of Horta.

💧 Flores

Waterfalls, lakes, dramatic cliffs, lush vegetation and some of the archipelago's most spectacular natural scenery.

☀️ Santa Maria

Known for warmer, drier landscapes, beaches, coastal scenery and a distinctive island character.

Wild Nature

Flores is especially appealing for travellers looking for waterfalls, lakes, cliffs and lush landscapes.

Beaches & Sunshine

Santa Maria offers a somewhat drier climate and attractive coastal landscapes for travellers looking for a different side of the Azores.

Atlantic Wildlife

Whales, dolphins & marine life

The Atlantic waters around the Azores make the archipelago an important destination for marine wildlife experiences.

🐋 Whale Watching

Boat excursions can offer opportunities to observe whales in their natural Atlantic environment.

🐬 Dolphins

Several dolphin species can be encountered around the islands during marine excursions.

🤿 Diving

Volcanic underwater landscapes and Atlantic marine life create distinctive diving experiences.

⛵ Ocean Trips

Boat tours provide another way to experience the islands from the water and discover their coastline.

🌊 Atlantic Ecosystem

The surrounding ocean is central to the culture, landscape and wildlife of the Azores.

📷 Nature Photography

Marine excursions and dramatic landscapes provide exceptional opportunities for nature photography.

🥩

Cozido das Furnas

Atlantic Fish

Azorean Cheese

🍍

São Miguel Pineapple

Local Cafés

Island Wines

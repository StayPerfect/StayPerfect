Why Ajaccio?

Mediterranean beauty with Corsican character.

Ajaccio is one of the best starting points for discovering the western coast of Corsica. The city combines a relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere with historic streets, beaches, restaurants, markets and beautiful coastal scenery.

It is also closely associated with Napoleon Bonaparte, who was born here in 1769. Today, visitors can combine history and culture with swimming, boat trips, local food and spectacular sunsets.

🏖️ Beaches Beautiful Mediterranean beaches and swimming spots
🏛️ History Napoleon's birthplace and historic old town
🌊 Coast Scenic bays, islands and coastal viewpoints
🍽️ Food Corsican cuisine, seafood, cheese and local products

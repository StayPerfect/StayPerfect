Why Cabrera & Dragonera?

Wild islands, protected nature and a different side of Mallorca.

Cabrera and Sa Dragonera offer a very different Mediterranean experience from the traditional beach-resort holiday. They are places for travellers who want nature, sea, wildlife, walking and spectacular landscapes.

Cabrera lies south of Mallorca and forms part of a maritime-terrestrial National Park, while Sa Dragonera is a protected natural park off Mallorca's western coast. Both are especially interesting for outdoor and nature-focused travellers. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Two Natural Escapes

Cabrera & Sa Dragonera

Although both islands belong to the Balearics, they offer very different landscapes and experiences.

National Park

Cabrera

The Cabrera Archipelago Maritime-Terrestrial National Park is located south of Mallorca and is known for protected landscapes, clear waters, marine biodiversity, beaches and the famous Cova Blava.

The archipelago includes Cabrera and numerous smaller islets. Its protected environment makes it particularly appealing for snorkelling, diving, hiking, birdwatching and boat excursions. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Nature Park

Sa Dragonera

Sa Dragonera is a rugged island off the western coast of Mallorca, separated from the mainland by a narrow channel and protected as a natural park.

Its landscape includes dramatic cliffs, hiking routes, historic lighthouses and rich birdlife. Access is by boat from several points on Mallorca, including Sant Elm. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Cabrera Highlights

A protected Mediterranean world.

Cabrera is one of the Balearics' most distinctive natural destinations. The archipelago became a Maritime-Terrestrial National Park in 1991 and protects an exceptionally rich combination of land and sea.

Among the highlights are the small natural harbour, the historic castle, the park visitor centre, beaches and the famous Cova Blava. The park is also particularly notable for its marine biodiversity. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

🏰 Cabrera Castle Historic landmark above the harbour
💙 Cova Blava Famous blue cave and marine attraction
🏖️ Beaches Quiet coves and clear waters
🐟 Marine Life Exceptional underwater biodiversity
Sa Dragonera Highlights

A rugged island facing Mallorca's western coast.

Sa Dragonera is a protected natural park covering the island of Dragonera together with the smaller islets of Pantaleu and sa Mitjana. The park is separated from Mallorca by around 800 metres.

Its approximately three-kilometre-long island includes historic lighthouses, a defence tower and trails with views over the Mallorcan coast. Access is by boat from several points on Mallorca. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

🏝️ Island Trails Explore the rugged natural landscape
💡 Lighthouses Historic lighthouse routes and viewpoints
🦅 Wildlife Important habitat for bird species
🌊 Marine Reserve Protected waters around the island

Discover Fuerteventura

The island for space, sea and freedom.

Fuerteventura is one of the Canary Islands' most distinctive destinations. Its enormous beaches, clear Atlantic waters, volcanic terrain and relaxed atmosphere make it ideal for travellers who want to combine nature, adventure and beach time.

Whether you want to surf, explore dunes, enjoy a quiet beach or simply drive through dramatic landscapes, Fuerteventura gives you room to travel at your own rhythm.

🏖️ Beaches Some of the Canary Islands' most spectacular beaches.
🏄 Adventure Surf, kitesurf, windsurf and outdoor activities.
🌋 Nature Volcanic landscapes, dunes and protected areas.
🚗 Road trips A great island to explore by car.

Travel your way

Things to do in Fuerteventura

Fuerteventura is especially strong for travellers who want a combination of beach, nature and outdoor experiences.

🏄

Surfing

Discover some of the island's best-known surf areas and enjoy the Atlantic from the water.

🪁

Kitesurfing

Wind and open beaches make Fuerteventura one of Europe's best-known destinations for wind sports.

🏜️

Dunes

Walk through the spectacular dunes around Corralejo and experience one of the island's signature landscapes.

🥾

Hiking

Explore volcanic terrain, mountain paths, coastal routes and protected natural landscapes.

🚗

Road Trips

Rent a car and discover beaches, villages and viewpoints across the island at your own pace.

🌅

Sunsets

Finish the day on the Atlantic coast with dramatic sunsets and relaxed seaside atmosphere.

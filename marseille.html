Why Marseille?

Mediterranean energy with centuries of history.

Marseille combines an ancient port, Mediterranean beaches, colourful streets, seafood restaurants, historic monuments and some of the most spectacular coastal scenery in France.

Whether you are looking for a beach escape, a cultural city break, outdoor adventures, great food or a relaxed Mediterranean holiday, Marseille offers a distinctly different side of France.

Which side of Marseille is for you?

Marseille's neighbourhoods and coastal areas offer very different experiences, from the historic port and colourful old streets to beaches, viewpoints and spectacular Mediterranean landscapes.

⚓ Old Port

The historic heart of Marseille, filled with boats, restaurants, cafés, waterfront views and easy access to many of the city's major attractions.

Explore Old Port →

🎨 Le Panier

One of Marseille's oldest areas, known for narrow streets, colourful buildings, small squares, cafés, galleries and a strong local atmosphere.

Explore Le Panier →

🥾 Calanques

Dramatic cliffs, turquoise water, hiking trails and hidden coves make the Calanques one of the region's most spectacular natural experiences.

Explore the Calanques →

🏖️ Catalans & Beaches

A natural choice for travellers looking for sun, swimming, seaside walks and a relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere close to central Marseille.

⛪ Notre-Dame de la Garde

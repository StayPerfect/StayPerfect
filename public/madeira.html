Why Madeira?

An island made for exploration.

Madeira combines dramatic mountains, lush vegetation, Atlantic coastline and charming towns in one compact destination.

It is particularly well suited to travellers who want nature, hiking, viewpoints, road trips, food and outdoor experiences without giving up comfortable accommodation and vibrant local life.

🌿 Nature Forests, valleys and dramatic mountain scenery
🥾 Hiking Levadas, peaks and coastal trails
🌊 Coast Cliffs, natural pools and Atlantic views
🍷 Food & Wine Madeiran cuisine and island wine traditions
Explore the Island

Where should you go in Madeira?

From Funchal to mountain peaks and coastal villages, Madeira offers very different experiences within a relatively small island.

🇵🇹 Funchal

Madeira's lively capital, known for its historic centre, harbour, restaurants, markets, gardens and ocean views.

🌊 Porto Moniz

A dramatic north-coast destination famous for volcanic scenery, natural swimming pools and spectacular ocean landscapes.

🌿 Santana

A beautiful northern area surrounded by green landscapes, mountains, traditional architecture and hiking opportunities.

🏔️ Pico do Arieiro

One of Madeira's most impressive mountain viewpoints and a gateway to some of the island's iconic high-altitude scenery.

🌅 Câmara de Lobos

A traditional fishing town close to Funchal with colourful streets, harbour views and a strong local atmosphere.

🏝️ Porto Santo

A nearby island known for its long sandy beach and a distinctly different Atlantic island experience.

Outdoor Madeira

Nature & landscapes

Madeira is one of Europe's most distinctive destinations for mountains, forests, valleys and dramatic Atlantic scenery.

🥾

Levada Walks

Follow Madeira's famous irrigation channels through forests, valleys and spectacular mountain landscapes.

🏔️

Mountain Peaks

Explore high-altitude landscapes and viewpoints around Madeira's mountain interior.

🌲

Laurisilva Forest

Discover Madeira's ancient subtropical forest and one of the island's most distinctive natural environments.

💧

Waterfalls

Discover waterfalls, streams and lush valleys throughout the island's wetter northern landscapes.

🌅

Viewpoints

Enjoy dramatic panoramas over mountains, villages, cliffs and the Atlantic Ocean.

🌿

Gardens

Madeira's mild climate supports colourful gardens and botanical spaces around Funchal and beyond.

Atlantic Madeira

Coast & beaches

Madeira's coastline is defined by cliffs, volcanic rock, natural pools and dramatic Atlantic views.

🌊

Natural Pools

Volcanic rock pools create some of Madeira's most distinctive places for swimming and relaxing by the ocean.

🏖️

Porto Santo Beach

For a more traditional sandy-beach experience, Porto Santo offers a long golden coastline.

🚗

Scenic Coastal Drives

⛵

Ocean Experiences

Island Culture

Culture & places

Madeira's identity is shaped by its maritime history, villages, architecture, gardens, traditions and Atlantic location.

🏛️

Funchal Old Town

Explore historic streets, traditional buildings, restaurants, shops and the atmosphere of Madeira's capital.

⛪

Churches & Heritage

Discover religious buildings and historic sites reflecting the island's Portuguese heritage.

🏠

Traditional Villages

Small communities around the island reveal a quieter side of Madeiran life and architecture.

🌺

Gardens & Flowers

Madeira's climate has created a strong tradition of gardens, flowers and botanical collections.

🎉

Local Festivals

Seasonal celebrations bring together food, music, flowers and Madeiran traditions.

⚓

Maritime Heritage

The Atlantic has played a central role in Madeira's history, economy and culture.

Taste Madeira

Food & wine

Madeiran cuisine combines Portuguese traditions with local island ingredients and Atlantic influences.

🐟

Fresh Seafood

Atlantic fish and seafood are an important part of the island's culinary identity.

🍷

Madeira Wine

Discover one of the island's most famous products and its long wine-making tradition.

🍢

Espetada

A traditional Madeiran dish associated with local meat, fire cooking and island dining.

🍞

Bolo do Caco

A traditional Madeiran bread commonly served with meals and local specialities.

Local Produce

Cafés & Restaurants

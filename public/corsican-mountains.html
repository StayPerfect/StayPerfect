Why the Corsican Mountains?

Wild landscapes in the heart of the Mediterranean.

Corsica is famous for its beaches, but its interior reveals a completely different island. High mountains, forests, rocky valleys, rivers and traditional villages create a dramatic landscape far from the busy coast.

For hikers, photographers, outdoor enthusiasts and travellers who simply want to slow down in nature, the Corsican mountains offer some of the most memorable experiences on the island.

⛰️ Mountains Rugged peaks and dramatic mountain landscapes
🥾 Hiking Trails through forests, valleys and high terrain
🌲 Forests Beautiful wooded landscapes and mountain scenery
🏘️ Villages Traditional Corsican villages and local culture

Plan Your Adventure

Mountain Travel Tips

A little preparation makes exploring Corsica's mountains much easier and more enjoyable.

🥾 Choose the right trail

Check distance, elevation, weather and difficulty before setting out. Mountain conditions can change quickly.

☀️ Start early

Early starts can provide cooler temperatures, better light for photography and more time to enjoy the landscape.

💧 Carry water

Bring enough water and supplies, especially when hiking away from towns and established facilities.

🌦️ Check the weather

Mountain weather can be very different from conditions on the coast, so always check the forecast before hiking.

👟 Wear suitable footwear

Comfortable hiking footwear is important on rocky, uneven and sometimes steep terrain.

📍 Respect the environment

Stay on appropriate trails, take your rubbish with you and help protect Corsica's fragile mountain environment.

Why Bonifacio?

A city carved between stone and sea.

Bonifacio sits at the southern tip of Corsica on a dramatic limestone peninsula. Its historic upper town rises above the Mediterranean, creating one of the island's most distinctive landscapes.

The destination brings together history, architecture, coastal adventure, beaches, boat trips and spectacular viewpoints. It is equally suited to a romantic escape, a cultural city break or an active Mediterranean holiday.

🏰 Citadel Medieval streets, fortifications and historic heritage
🌊 Coast White limestone cliffs and turquoise Mediterranean waters
⛵ Marina Restaurants, boats and a lively waterfront atmosphere
🏝️ Lavezzi Wild islands and clear waters south of Bonifacio
Discover Bonifacio

Places you should not miss

Explore the historic heart of Bonifacio and the spectacular coastline surrounding the town.

🏰 Bonifacio Citadel

Walk through the medieval upper town, explore the old fortifications and discover narrow streets overlooking the cliffs and Mediterranean.

🌊 Limestone Cliffs

Bonifacio's bright limestone cliffs rise dramatically above the sea and create extraordinary viewpoints from land and water.

⛵ Marina

The port is the natural gateway to Bonifacio, with boats, restaurants, cafés and views toward the dramatic citadel.

🪨 King of Aragon's Staircase

One of Bonifacio's most famous landmarks, the spectacular staircase is carved into the cliff and descends dramatically toward the sea.

🏘️ Old Town

Lose yourself among narrow cobbled streets, historic houses, small squares, churches, cafés, boutiques and panoramic viewpoints.

🏝️ Lavezzi Islands

Take a boat from Bonifacio toward the Lavezzi archipelago, known for granite landscapes, clear water and beautiful Mediterranean scenery.

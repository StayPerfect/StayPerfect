Why Toulouse?

A warmer, slower side of France.

Toulouse is one of southern France's most distinctive cities, famous for its pink-toned brick buildings, historic centre, riverside atmosphere and connection to the aerospace industry.

It is an excellent destination for travellers looking for culture, food, architecture, history and a more relaxed city experience away from the busiest tourist routes.

🏛️ Place du Capitole The symbolic heart of Toulouse
🌊 Garonne Beautiful riverside walks and views
🚀 Aerospace Airbus and space exploration
🍷 Southern Cuisine Regional food and relaxed dining

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how StayPerfect handles information when you visit the StayPerfect website and use links to external travel services.

StayPerfect is primarily an informational and travel-discovery website. We do not require you to create an account in order to browse the website.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address and the contents of your message. We use that information to respond to your request and communicate with you.

Like most websites, StayPerfect may be hosted on infrastructure that processes basic technical information needed to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and timestamps.

3. Affiliate links

Some links on StayPerfect are affiliate links. When you click an affiliate link, the external partner may be able to associate your visit with StayPerfect for referral tracking and commissions.

Examples of external travel services linked from StayPerfect may include Klook, KKday, Aviasales, Auto Europe, EconomyBookings, Intui, GetTransfer, Saily, Compensair, BikesBooking, WeGoTrip and Ekta.

When you leave StayPerfect and visit one of these services, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

StayPerfect uses Travelpayouts affiliate technology and tracking links to measure referrals from the website. These technologies may use identifiers, cookies or similar mechanisms to attribute visits and qualifying purchases to the appropriate affiliate partner.

Travelpayouts and the relevant partner websites may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

StayPerfect contains links to third-party websites and services. We do not control those websites and are not responsible for their privacy practices, security, content or data-processing activities.

Before providing personal information to an external service, we recommend reviewing that service's privacy policy.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website, provide hosting, security, email communication, affiliate tracking or other website functions.

8. Data retention

Information sent to us directly by email is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain relevant communications or meet applicable legal obligations.

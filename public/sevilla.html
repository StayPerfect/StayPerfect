Welcome to Seville

The soul of Andalusia.

Seville is a city where centuries of history, architecture and Andalusian culture come together. The historic centre brings together the Cathedral, Giralda and Real Alcázar within a remarkably compact area.

Spend your morning exploring royal palaces and historic squares, walk through Santa Cruz in the afternoon and cross the Guadalquivir towards Triana as the city begins to glow at sunset.

At night, discover traditional tapas, local cuisine and the unmistakable rhythm of flamenco.

Must See

Seville highlights

Begin with the landmarks that define the historic and cultural identity of Seville.

Real Alcázar

Explore one of Europe's oldest royal palace complexes, known for remarkable architecture, courtyards and gardens.

Cathedral & Giralda

Discover Seville Cathedral and the famous Giralda, one of the city's most recognisable landmarks.

Plaza de España

Admire the monumental semicircular square, colourful ceramic details, bridges and canals.

Explore

Seville's neighborhoods

Discover the different personalities that make Seville more than just its famous monuments.

Santa Cruz

A historic neighbourhood of narrow streets, flower-filled courtyards, plazas and traditional Andalusian architecture.

Triana

Famous for ceramics, colourful riverside streets, flamenco traditions and lively local atmosphere.

El Arenal

A central area close to the river, Cathedral, Torre del Oro and traditional restaurants.

Alameda

A lively area known for restaurants, cafés, nightlife and a more alternative side of Seville.

Macarena

A historic neighbourhood offering local atmosphere, traditional architecture and cultural sites.

Centro

The central shopping and historic area where visitors can explore many sights on foot.

Green Seville

María Luisa Park.

María Luisa Park is one of Seville's most beautiful green spaces and provides a peaceful contrast to the historic centre.

Wander beneath the trees, discover fountains and gardens, and continue towards the spectacular Plaza de España.

It is an ideal place to slow down during a busy day of sightseeing.

Seville

The Guadalquivir at sunset.

Finish the day beside the river and enjoy the atmosphere of Seville as the historic city lights begin to appear.

Travel Tips

Before you go

A few simple tips will help you make the most of your Seville experience.

Best time to visit

Spring and autumn are especially attractive for walking, sightseeing and enjoying outdoor spaces.

Getting around

Much of central Seville is easy to explore on foot, with public transport available for longer distances.

How long to stay

Three days is a good starting point, while four days allows more time for neighbourhoods and experiences.

Explore slowly

Leave time for tapas, evening walks, plazas and the relaxed rhythm of Andalusian life.

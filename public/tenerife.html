Why Tenerife?

One island. Many ways to travel.

Tenerife is one of the most diverse destinations in the Canary Islands. You can combine beaches and ocean views with volcanic landscapes, mountain adventures, local food, nightlife and peaceful nature.

That variety makes Tenerife particularly interesting for travellers who don't want to choose between a beach holiday and an active escape.

✓ Great for beach and nature lovers
✓ Excellent hiking and outdoor opportunities
✓ Suitable for couples, families, friends and solo travellers
✓ Large variety of accommodation and activities

Iconic Tenerife

Mount Teide 🌋

Mount Teide is the defining landscape of Tenerife. The volcanic environment creates an experience completely different from the island's beaches and coastal resorts.

For travellers who enjoy nature, photography, hiking and dramatic landscapes, this is one of the experiences that should be high on the list.

Where to stay

Different areas, different Tenerife

The best area depends on the kind of trip you want. Here are some of the island's most useful bases.

🌴

Costa Adeje

A popular choice for beaches, resorts, restaurants, comfort and a polished holiday atmosphere.

🌊

Los Cristianos

A convenient coastal base with beaches, restaurants, shops and easy access to excursions.

🎉

Playa de las Américas

A lively area suited to travellers looking for nightlife, entertainment, restaurants and activities.

🌿

Puerto de la Cruz

A greener and more traditional-feeling option with gardens, volcanic scenery and northern Tenerife atmosphere.

🏔️

La Orotava

A beautiful historic area close to mountain landscapes, traditional architecture and the Teide region.

🌅

Garachico

A quieter historic coastal destination ideal for travellers who prefer scenery, culture and a slower rhythm.

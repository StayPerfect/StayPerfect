Welcome to Valencia

Where Mediterranean life meets modern Spain.

Valencia is one of Spain's most fascinating cities, combining centuries of history with bold contemporary architecture and a relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere.

Wander through the historic centre, visit the Central Market and Silk Exchange, cycle through the Turia Gardens, and continue towards the spectacular City of Arts and Sciences.

When the day slows down, head towards the coast for fresh seafood, Valencian paella and a sunset beside the Mediterranean.

Beyond the City

Discover Albufera.

Just outside Valencia lies the Albufera Natural Park, a peaceful landscape of wetlands, rice fields, traditional boats and Mediterranean nature.

The area is closely connected with Valencian rice cultivation and the culinary traditions surrounding paella.

A sunset excursion to Albufera is one of the best ways to experience the natural side of Valencia.

Travel Tips

Before you go

A few simple tips can help you get the most from your Valencia trip.

Best time to visit

Spring and autumn are particularly pleasant for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Getting around

Valencia is relatively flat and easy to explore on foot, by bicycle, metro and public transport.

How long to stay

Three days is a good starting point, while four or five days allows time for beaches and Albufera.

Take your time

Leave space for long lunches, evening walks and relaxed Mediterranean moments.

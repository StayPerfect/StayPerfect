Why France?

One country, countless ways to travel.

France combines romantic cities, Mediterranean beaches, Alpine mountains, countryside villages, historic landmarks and one of the world's most celebrated food and wine cultures.

Whether you want to explore Paris, relax on the Côte d'Azur, discover Provence, ski in the Alps, enjoy French cuisine or take a romantic escape, France makes it possible to build a trip around your own style.

🗼 Cities Paris, Lyon, Bordeaux and historic centres
🏖️ Coast French Riviera and Atlantic beaches
⛰️ Mountains Alps, Pyrenees and spectacular landscapes
🍷 Food French cuisine, pastries, cheese and wine

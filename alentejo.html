Why Alentejo?

Portugal at a slower pace.

Alentejo is one of Portugal's best choices for travellers looking for space, authenticity and a more relaxed rhythm. Wide landscapes, cork oak forests, vineyards, historic villages and traditional food define much of the region.

From Évora and the medieval villages of the interior to the Atlantic coast around Comporta and the Alentejo coastline, the region combines culture, nature, food and beaches in one destination.

🏛️ History Évora, castles, villages and heritage
🍷 Wine Vineyards and regional wine experiences
🏖️ Coast Atlantic beaches and dramatic coastline
🌾 Countryside Open landscapes, cork oaks and rural life
Explore Alentejo

Where should you go?

From historic inland towns to Atlantic beaches, Alentejo offers different experiences depending on how you want to travel.

🏛️ Évora

A UNESCO-listed historic city filled with Roman, medieval and traditional Portuguese heritage, atmospheric streets and local food.

🏰 Monsaraz

A hilltop medieval village overlooking the Alqueva landscape, ideal for history, views, countryside and peaceful evenings.

⛰️ Marvão

A dramatic hilltop village and castle surrounded by the landscapes of northern Alentejo.

🌾 Comporta

A relaxed coastal destination known for beaches, dunes, rice fields, nature and a sophisticated but understated atmosphere.

🌊 Alentejo Coast

Wild Atlantic beaches, cliffs, small coastal towns and scenic landscapes make the coast ideal for road trips and nature escapes.

🍷 Wine Country

Discover vineyards, wineries, olive groves and traditional countryside across the interior of Alentejo.

Alqueva

The Alentejo coastline is one of the region's most distinctive experiences, with long beaches, dunes, cliffs, fishing villages and protected natural landscapes.

It works especially well as part of a road trip, combining coastal days with inland villages, vineyards and countryside.

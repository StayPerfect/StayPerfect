Why Annecy?

A perfect meeting point between lake and mountains.

Annecy is one of France's most picturesque destinations, where a beautiful historic centre meets the clear waters of Lake Annecy and the dramatic French Alps.

Whether you want a romantic escape, an active outdoor holiday, a relaxing lakeside break, a family adventure or a scenic French getaway, Annecy offers an exceptional combination of nature, culture and atmosphere.

🏔️ French Alps Mountain scenery in every direction
💧 Lake Annecy Clear water and beautiful lakeside views
🏘️ Old Town Canals, bridges and historic streets
🚲 Outdoor Life Cycling, hiking, boating and adventure

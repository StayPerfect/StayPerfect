Why Asturias?

Spain's green northern escape.

Asturias offers a completely different side of Spain. Instead of the dry Mediterranean landscapes often associated with the country, here you'll find lush green valleys, rugged mountains, dramatic Atlantic cliffs and peaceful coastal villages.

It is an ideal destination for travellers looking for nature, authentic food, outdoor adventures and a slower pace of travel.

🌿 Green Landscapes Mountains, forests and valleys
🌊 Atlantic Coast Cliffs, coves and wild beaches
⛰️ Picos de Europa Dramatic mountain scenery
🍎 Asturian Cider A local tradition worth discovering

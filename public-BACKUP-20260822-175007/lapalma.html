Why La Palma?

An island for travellers who want nature.

La Palma combines dramatic volcanic scenery, lush forests, hiking routes, peaceful villages and Atlantic landscapes. It is especially suited to travellers looking for outdoor experiences and a slower, more authentic island escape.

🌿

Wild & green

La Palma is one of the greenest islands in the Canary archipelago, with forests, deep valleys, volcanic landscapes and mountain scenery.

🥾

Made for exploration

From short walks to challenging mountain routes, the island offers a wide variety of ways to discover its landscapes.

🌌

A world-class sky

The island's high-altitude locations and protected skies make it a remarkable destination for astronomy and stargazing.

🌊

Atlantic escapes

Discover rugged coastlines, natural swimming pools, ocean viewpoints and peaceful places away from the crowds.

Travel style

Why choose La Palma?

Not every island is about nightlife and large resorts. La Palma offers a different kind of Canary Islands experience.

🌋 Volcanic adventure

Explore a landscape shaped by volcanic activity, where mountains, lava formations and dramatic terrain create a destination unlike a traditional beach holiday.

🌲 Deep connection with nature

Forests, mountain trails and protected natural areas make the island particularly attractive to outdoor travellers.

🌌 Incredible night skies

When the sun goes down, La Palma becomes a destination for astronomy and stargazing, adding another dimension to the trip.

🌊 Peaceful Atlantic atmosphere

The island provides a slower alternative to some of the busier Canary Islands, with coastal villages and quieter landscapes.

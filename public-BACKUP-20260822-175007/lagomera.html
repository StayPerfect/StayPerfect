Why La Gomera?

Small island. Big landscapes.

La Gomera is especially suited to travellers who want nature, hiking, peaceful beaches and an authentic island atmosphere rather than large-scale resort tourism.

🌿

A paradise for nature lovers

At the heart of the island lies Garajonay National Park, famous for its ancient laurel forest and extensive network of trails. The park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🥾

Made for walking

La Gomera offers a huge network of paths connecting forests, mountains, valleys, villages and the coast. It is one of the Canary Islands' strongest destinations for hiking.

The island's natural heart

Garajonay National Park 🌿

The centrepiece of La Gomera is its UNESCO-listed Garajonay National Park, home to ancient laurel forest and a network of trails crossing one of the island's most distinctive landscapes.

For travellers who love hiking, forests and dramatic scenery, this is one of the strongest reasons to choose La Gomera.

Things to do

Experience La Gomera your way

The island is ideal for active travellers, nature lovers and anyone looking for a slower Atlantic escape.

🥾

Hiking

Explore trails through Garajonay, valleys, ravines and coastal landscapes. La Gomera has an extensive network of paths across the island.

🌊

Black-sand beaches

Discover volcanic beaches and small coastal spots surrounded by cliffs and dramatic Atlantic scenery.

🐋

Whale & dolphin watching

The southern coast offers opportunities to discover marine life and experience La Gomera from the Atlantic.

🚲

Cycling

Mountain landscapes and winding roads create an excellent setting for cyclists looking for a more active island experience.

🌅

Sunset by the ocean

Valle Gran Rey and other western coastal areas are ideal for slowing down and enjoying the Atlantic at the end of the day.

🎶

Discover local culture

Experience traditional villages, island cuisine and the famous Silbo Gomero whistled language.

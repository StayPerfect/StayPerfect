Why Galicia?

Spain's wild Atlantic side.

Galicia feels different from the Mediterranean Spain that many travellers know. The landscape is greener, the Atlantic coast is dramatic and the region has a strong identity shaped by centuries of maritime culture and tradition.

From Santiago de Compostela and its famous pilgrimage route to the Rías Baixas, Costa da Morte and the dramatic cliffs of the Atlantic coast, Galicia is ideal for travellers looking for nature, culture, food and authentic experiences.

🌊 Atlantic Coast Dramatic cliffs, beaches and fishing villages
🍤 Seafood Fresh shellfish, octopus and Atlantic cuisine
⛪ Santiago Historic streets and the Camino de Santiago
🌿 Nature Green countryside, forests and coastal landscapes

Where should you go in Galicia?

Discover Galicia through its historic cities, Atlantic coast, beautiful estuaries and traditional seaside destinations.

⛪ Santiago de Compostela

The historic heart of Galicia and the destination of the famous Camino de Santiago. Explore its cathedral, atmospheric old town, squares and traditional Galician restaurants.

Explore Santiago →

🌊 A Coruña

A lively Atlantic city surrounded by the sea, known for its beaches, promenade, seafood, historic centre and the Tower of Hercules.

Explore A Coruña →

⛵ Vigo

A major Atlantic port with access to the spectacular Rías Baixas, fresh seafood, coastal views and the nearby Cíes Islands.

Explore Vigo →

🌊 Rías Baixas

Beautiful estuaries, sandy beaches, seafood villages and vineyards make the Rías Baixas one of Galicia's most rewarding coastal areas.

🌊 Costa da Morte

One of Galicia's wildest coastal areas, with dramatic cliffs, lighthouses, remote beaches and spectacular Atlantic scenery.

Galician Experiences

What can you experience?

Galicia is a destination where the journey itself becomes part of the experience.

⛪

Santiago de Compostela

Walk through the historic centre, visit the cathedral and experience the atmosphere of one of Europe's great pilgrimage cities.

🥾

Camino de Santiago

Walk a section of the legendary pilgrimage route and discover villages, countryside and centuries of history along the way.

🦪

Galician Seafood

Taste oysters, mussels, prawns, octopus, scallops and other fresh seafood from the Atlantic.

🍷

Rías Baixas Wine

Explore the wine culture of the Rías Baixas and discover the region's famous Albariño wines.

🏝️

Cíes Islands

Discover spectacular beaches, clear Atlantic waters and protected natural landscapes around the Cíes Islands.

🌅

Atlantic Sunsets

Watch the sun disappear over the Atlantic from beaches, cliffs, lighthouses and coastal viewpoints.

🏰

Historic Towns

Explore medieval streets, Roman heritage, churches, squares and traditional Galician villages.

🚗

Coastal Road Trips

Drive between fishing villages, beaches, lighthouses and spectacular viewpoints along the Galician coast.

🌿

Galician Nature

Discover green countryside, forests, rivers, estuaries, cliffs and protected natural landscapes.

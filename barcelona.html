Suggested itinerary

Barcelona in 4 days

A balanced first visit combining architecture, historic neighbourhoods, culture and the Mediterranean.

DAY 01

Gaudí & central Barcelona

Start with Sagrada Família, continue towards Passeig de Gràcia and explore Casa Batlló and La Pedrera. Finish with dinner in the city centre.

DAY 02

Park Güell & old Barcelona

Visit Park Güell in the morning, then explore the Gothic Quarter, La Rambla and El Born.

DAY 03

Montjuïc & culture

Explore Montjuïc, its viewpoints, gardens and museums before spending the evening discovering Barcelona's food scene.

DAY 04

Mediterranean Barcelona

Slow the pace down with Barceloneta, the waterfront and a relaxed final day by the Mediterranean.

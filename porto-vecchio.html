Why Porto-Vecchio?

Where turquoise water meets the mountains.

Porto-Vecchio is one of the key destinations in southern Corsica, combining a historic town with spectacular beaches, Mediterranean landscapes and easy access to the island's mountain interior.

It is a particularly flexible destination: you can spend the morning on the beach, explore the old town in the afternoon and head toward the mountains for hiking and nature experiences.

🏖️ Beaches Beautiful sandy beaches and clear Mediterranean water
🏰 Citadel Historic centre overlooking the Gulf of Porto-Vecchio
🌲 Nature Pine forests, countryside and protected landscapes
⛰️ Mountains Easy access toward Alta Rocca and the Bavella massif
Discover Porto-Vecchio

Places you should not miss

Explore the beaches, historic centre and natural landscapes that make southern Corsica so special.

🏰 Porto-Vecchio Citadel

Explore the historic citadel, atmospheric streets, cafés, shops and viewpoints overlooking the town and gulf.

🏖️ Palombaggia

One of southern Corsica's best-known beach areas, with fine sand, clear water and a landscape framed by pine trees.

🌊 Santa Giulia

A beautiful lagoon-like bay with shallow turquoise water, sandy beaches and a relaxed Mediterranean atmosphere.

⛵ Gulf of Porto-Vecchio

Discover the coastline from the water with boat trips, hidden coves and beautiful views across the Mediterranean.

⛰️ Alta Rocca

Head inland toward forests, mountain villages and dramatic landscapes around the Bavella and Ospedale massifs.

🏔️ Bavella

Discover one of Corsica's most spectacular mountain areas, with granite peaks, forests, hiking routes and panoramic views.

Porto-Vecchio Experiences

What can you experience?

From beach days to mountain adventures, Porto-Vecchio gives you many ways to experience southern Corsica.

🏖️

Beach Day

Spend the day between fine sand, turquoise water and pine-fringed Mediterranean beaches.

⛵

Boat Trip

Explore the coastline from the sea and discover coves, beaches and landscapes that are difficult to reach by road.

🏰

Historic Centre

Walk through the citadel, discover Corsican heritage and enjoy restaurants, cafés and local shops.

🥾

Mountain Hiking

Travel inland toward Alta Rocca and discover forests, mountain paths and spectacular Corsican landscapes.

🌅

Sunset by the Sea

Finish the day with a Mediterranean sunset overlooking the gulf or one of the beaches around Porto-Vecchio.

🍷

Corsican Food

Taste local specialities, regional products, seafood, cheeses and traditional Corsican cuisine.

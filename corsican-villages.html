The Authentic Side of Corsica

Discover the island beyond the coast.

Corsica is famous for turquoise water and beautiful beaches, but some of its most memorable places are hidden in the mountains. Traditional villages sit among dramatic peaks, forests and valleys, offering a slower and more authentic way to experience the island.

Wander through stone streets, stop at a local café, taste regional specialities and enjoy panoramic views across the Corsican landscape. The villages are perfect for travellers who want culture, nature and local atmosphere in one trip.

🏘️ Stone Villages Historic houses, narrow streets and traditional architecture
⛰️ Mountain Views Dramatic landscapes and panoramic viewpoints
🧀 Local Food Cheese, charcuterie, chestnuts and Corsican cuisine
🌿 Slow Travel Peaceful villages and authentic island experiences

Village Experiences

What to do in Corsican villages

The best way to experience Corsica's villages is to slow down, explore on foot and let the landscape and local culture become part of the journey.

🥾

Walk Historic Streets

Explore narrow stone lanes, old houses, churches, small squares and hidden viewpoints on foot.

📸

Scenic Viewpoints

Many villages sit high above valleys or the sea, creating spectacular places for photography and unforgettable views.

🧀

Taste Local Food

Try traditional Corsican cheese, charcuterie, chestnut products, local pastries and regional specialities.

☕

Slow Down

Stop for coffee, enjoy a local restaurant and experience the relaxed rhythm of village life.

🌲

Explore Nature

Use mountain villages as starting points for hiking, forests, valleys, rivers and spectacular natural landscapes.

🚗

Scenic Road Trips

Drive between villages and discover winding mountain roads, coastal viewpoints and landscapes that are difficult to reach without a car.

Why Provence?

One region, countless ways to experience it.

Provence is one of southern France's most distinctive destinations, combining medieval villages, Roman heritage, lavender landscapes, vineyards, markets, Mediterranean sunshine and a celebrated food culture.

Whether you are looking for a romantic escape, a road trip through the countryside, a food-focused holiday, art and history, a family adventure or simply a beautiful place to slow down, Provence offers a memorable experience at almost every turn.

💜 Lavender Fields, fragrances and iconic summer landscapes
🏘️ Villages Hilltop villages, stone houses and historic streets
🍷 Food & Wine Markets, olive oil, wine and Provençal cuisine
☀️ Mediterranean Sunshine, coastlines and relaxed southern living

Provence is made up of distinctive landscapes and destinations, each with its own character, architecture, food, scenery and experiences.

🏛️ Avignon

Discover historic Provence through the Palais des Papes, medieval streets, lively squares, cultural events and the surrounding Rhône landscape.

Explore Avignon →

🎨 Aix-en-Provence

An elegant Provençal city known for fountains, cafés, markets, architecture, art and the legacy of painter Paul Cézanne.

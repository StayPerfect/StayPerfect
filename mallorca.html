Why Mallorca?

One island, countless ways to travel.

Mallorca combines Mediterranean beaches with mountains, historic towns, rural landscapes and a lively capital. The island's variety makes it possible to build very different holidays without leaving the same destination.

From Palma and the beaches of the south to the Serra de Tramuntana and the coves of the east, Mallorca can work for families, couples, friends, outdoor travellers and anyone looking for a slower Mediterranean escape.

🏖️ Beaches Long sandy beaches and turquoise coves
⛰️ Mountains Serra de Tramuntana landscapes and trails
🏛️ Culture Palma, villages and Mediterranean heritage
🍊 Food Local products and Mediterranean gastronomy

Mediterranean Coast

Mallorca beaches & coves

Mallorca's coastline ranges from enormous sandy beaches to small coves surrounded by cliffs and Mediterranean vegetation.

🏖️

Es Trenc

One of the island's best-known sandy beach areas, famous for its turquoise water and open Mediterranean landscape.

💙

Cala Mondragó

A scenic southeastern coastal area combining clear water, sandy beaches and natural surroundings.

🌊

Playa de Muro

A long sandy beach in the north, close to the S'Albufera wetland and well suited to relaxed beach days.

🏝️

Tramuntana Coves

Discover dramatic coastal scenery and secluded swimming spots along the mountainous northwestern coast.

City & Culture

Palma — Mallorca's Mediterranean capital

Palma combines Mediterranean city life with architecture, culture, gastronomy, shopping and easy access to the rest of the island.

It can work as a destination on its own or as a convenient base for exploring beaches, mountains, villages and other parts of Mallorca.

Serra de Tramuntana

The Serra de Tramuntana stretches across Mallorca's northwest, combining mountain scenery, traditional villages, hiking routes and spectacular coastal landscapes.
